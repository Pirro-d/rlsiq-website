How often should fire doors be inspected?→

NFPA 80 generally calls for inspection and testing of fire door assemblies at least annually. Applicable local requirements may vary.

What does a fire door inspection include?→

Inspections typically evaluate the door and frame, labels, hinges, hardware, clearances, closing and latching operation, and other applicable conditions.

Can inspection records be organized digitally?→

Yes. Organized digital records can help facilities locate door information, review findings, and track follow-up work.

What happens when a deficiency is found?→

Findings are documented so facility teams can arrange appropriate corrective action and any necessary reinspection.
